The idea

Make the signal visible.

{{ lesson.note }} In an interview, narrate the observation before reaching for implementation details. That lets the interviewer evaluate your reasoning, not just your final code.

A useful sequence

  1. State the constraint you are responding to.
  2. Name the invariant or guarantee you want to preserve.
  3. Walk through one ordinary case and one edge case.
  4. Close with complexity, risk, and the next improvement.
